Cookie Preferences

Last updated: 24 July 2026

What this site uses

This website does not set any of its own tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies. There's no cart, no account system, and no cookie consent banner to manage, because there's currently nothing optional to opt in or out of.

Third-party embeds

When you use the "Upload your design" button on a product page, it opens Cloudinary's upload tool inside the page. While that upload window is open, Cloudinary may set its own cookies or use local storage to make the upload work — this is governed by Cloudinary's own privacy policy, not ours.

WhatsApp links

The "Find Out More on WhatsApp" buttons simply open a WhatsApp conversation in your browser or app — no cookies are set on this site as part of that, though WhatsApp/Meta may set their own once you're on their platform, under their own policy.

If this changes

If we add analytics or advertising tools to this site in the future, we'll update this page and add a proper cookie consent banner at that point, in line with UK/EU cookie law requirements.

Questions

If you have any questions about cookies on this site, contact crystal.embroidery@icloud.com.
