What we do

Our services

Three disciplines, all handled under one roof in Raunds.

Embroidery

Logos, crests and lettering stitched onto garments, caps, bags and patches. Flat, tubular and cap-frame work run on our own machines.

Workwear · Teamwear · Patches

Printing

Vinyl, DTF and sublimation for apparel, mugs and hard goods — the right choice for fine detail, photographic artwork and short runs.

DTF · Vinyl · Sublimation

Digitising

Your artwork converted into a production-ready stitch file, test-sewn and adjusted before it goes anywhere near your garments. Kept on file for reorders.

Test-sewn · Kept on file
How it works

From artwork to finished garment

Send your artwork

Upload a logo on any product page or send it over on WhatsApp. We'll advise on the best method for your garment and quantity.

Approve your proof

We digitise the design and send a digital proof. Nothing goes into production until you're happy with it.

Made in-store

Everything is produced on-site by our resident expert. Typically 7–10 days from proof approval, depending on workload.
